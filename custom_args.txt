. Custom launch options for scrcpy Launcher
. Format: Label = --arg1 --arg2 ...
. Each line becomes a checkbox in the Launch section.
. Blank lines and lines starting with . are ignored.
.
. Use {default_value} to add an editable text box next to the checkbox.
. The value in the text box replaces the {placeholder} at launch time.
.
. Examples (uncomment to use):
. No Audio = --no-audio
. Camera Mode = --video-source=camera
Low Latency = --video-buffer={50} --audio-buffer={50}
. Borderless = --window-borderless
. Fullscreen = --fullscreen
. Max FPS = --max-fps={60}
. Crop = --crop={1080:960:0:0}
